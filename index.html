Privacy Policy

We believe your morning routine should be yours. This policy explains how ROARISE handles information when you use our motivational alarm-clock app, contact support, or purchase a subscription.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

At a glance: ROARISE is designed around alarms and prerecorded motivational audio stored on your device. An account is not required for the core experience. Alarm times, preferred voices and modes are handled locally and through Apple's operating-system alarm services.

ROARISE does not need access to your microphone, photos, contacts, precise location or health data to operate its planned core features. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

Important: Subscription functionality is still being finalized. The applicable disclosures and Apple App Privacy answers must be checked against the exact version released, including any RevenueCat SDK enabled in that version.

1. Who we are and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to ROARISE (bundle identifier com.roarise.alarm), an iOS motivational alarm-clock application published by an independent developer under the ROARISE name (“ROARISE,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

For privacy inquiries and requests relating to information we control, contact us at z68827163@gmail.com. You can also use our Support and Privacy Requests page.

Where European data-protection law applies, the publisher of ROARISE is responsible for the personal data it determines the purposes and means of processing. Apple and other providers may also act as independent controllers for services they offer directly. Requests to those providers may need to be addressed to them.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the ROARISE iOS app, its associated public support/privacy pages, communications with our support contact, and in-app purchases or subscriptions once enabled. It does not replace Apple's own privacy notices for iOS, App Store purchases or Apple Accounts.

ROARISE uses prerecorded voice messages. Choosing a voice in ROARISE does not involve recording your voice, transmitting your microphone input, or sending personal wake-up prompts to an AI service as part of the planned core alarm experience. Audio production by the developer is separate from user data processing in the app.

3. Information handled

3.1 Information stored or managed on your device

To provide alarm functionality and remember your choices, the app may save the following locally:

  • Alarm times and scheduling information, associated alarm identifiers, and local status or recovery information.
  • Selected motivational mode, voice, King/Queen Energy preference, sound preference, and onboarding completion state.
  • Bundled prerecorded sound assets, integrity metadata, and other settings necessary to present, preview, schedule, edit, or cancel alarms.
  • Limited local operational state used to avoid duplicate scheduling and recover from interrupted alarm edits.

AlarmKit is provided by Apple. The iOS system may maintain alarm configuration and authorization state needed to show and deliver alarms. This is operating-system functionality and is subject to Apple's platform privacy practices.

We do not operate a ROARISE account database or require an email address, password, photo, microphone recording, contact list, precise location, or health record to use the planned core alarm functionality.

3.2 Purchases and subscriptions (when enabled)

If a release offers ROARISE Pro, purchases, trials, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are processed through Apple's App Store and Apple Account infrastructure. The app may receive transaction identifiers, product identifiers, subscription entitlement status, purchase and expiry dates, and other purchase-related information necessary to provide, restore, and validate access. We do not receive your complete payment-card number.

If ROARISE enables RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements, RevenueCat may process a pseudonymous or anonymous app user identifier, purchase/receipt and transaction information, subscription status, app and device technical information, and limited service activity required for entitlement management and troubleshooting. An anonymous identifier is not necessarily equivalent to data that cannot be personal data under all privacy laws. We will confirm and disclose the actual SDK configuration before release. Purchase history may be retained by Apple and subscription providers under their own applicable requirements.

3.3 Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, device model, iOS version, app version, and the details of your support issue. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, medical information, or other sensitive information unless strictly necessary.

3.4 Diagnostics and analytics

We do not currently plan to add third-party advertising or behavioral analytics trackers to the core app. Apple may make crash or diagnostic information available to developers when users choose to share such information through their Apple device settings. If we later integrate additional diagnostics or analytics providers, we will update this policy and App Store privacy disclosures before that change is released.

5. Third parties and platform services

  • Apple / iOS / AlarmKit: operating-system alarm scheduling, authorization and system alarm controls. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: subscription purchases, payment processing, billing, trial eligibility, renewal, refund and purchase restoration when implemented.
  • RevenueCat (only if included in a released version): subscription entitlement validation and purchase management, as described in Section 3.2. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of this publicly accessible policy and support website; GitHub may process connection and access information under its own policies. GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Email provider: our support email service processes communications you send to us and related transmission metadata.

We do not provide personal user voice recordings to ElevenLabs: the motivational audio offered in the app is prerecorded developer-created material. The mere presence of a synthetic voice in ROARISE does not mean your personal data is sent to that provider.

6. Sharing, sale and tracking

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share your app activity with data brokers or third-party advertising networks, and we do not use cross-app tracking for targeted advertising.

Information may be disclosed to the platform and processors described above only as needed to operate the relevant features, respond to your requests, comply with law, protect rights or security, or perform a lawful business transition with appropriate safeguards.

7. Storage, retention and deletion

  • Alarm and preferences: saved on your device and, where applicable, managed by iOS AlarmKit until you change/delete alarms, clear app information, or remove the app. Uninstalling may not cancel a paid App Store subscription; use Apple's subscription management separately.
  • Purchases: Apple and any enabled entitlement provider retain transaction and subscription records according to applicable law, contracts and their retention policies. App deletion does not necessarily delete these records.
  • Support communications: retained only for the time reasonably needed to resolve your request, handle follow-up questions, protect legitimate interests, or satisfy legal requirements; records are reviewed for deletion when no longer needed.
  • Legal records: information that must be retained for accounting, dispute resolution, fraud prevention or other legal obligations may be kept for the required period.

ROARISE does not currently provide a cloud account containing your alarm history. To remove locally stored preferences, you can delete the app and its local data using iOS controls, subject to iOS handling of system alarms. You should separately inspect and remove scheduled alarms if applicable. For data we or our service providers control, email us with a privacy request; we will explain what can be deleted and what records must lawfully remain.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where used, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights for information controlled by ROARISE, email z68827163@gmail.com with the subject “ROARISE Privacy Request.” Explain your request and, if necessary, provide enough information to locate the relevant correspondence or purchase record. Do not send unnecessary identity documents. We may request proportionate verification to protect against unauthorized disclosures.

For eligible GDPR requests, we aim to respond within one month, subject to lawful extensions. You can contact your local data-protection authority; users in Denmark may contact Datatilsynet.

To change or cancel a subscription, use Apple's subscription management instructions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Payment disputes or refunds may require contacting Apple.

9. Security

ROARISE is designed to keep alarm and preference information on the device where practical. We use platform-provided safeguards and seek to limit collection and third-party access. No system or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure; please avoid including sensitive information in routine support emails.

10. International processing

Service providers, including Apple, GitHub, the email provider and any enabled subscription provider, may process information in different jurisdictions. Where required by applicable law, international transfers will rely on an appropriate legal basis or transfer safeguard. Provider policies describe their own processing and geographic arrangements.

11. Children

ROARISE is not designed specifically for children. The intended audience is users able to understand alarm and subscription settings. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through a ROARISE account-registration service. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so that we can evaluate the request and take appropriate action. Applicable App Store age ratings and parental controls remain governed by Apple's platform settings.
