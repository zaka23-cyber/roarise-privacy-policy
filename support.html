Alarms & Wake-Up Audio

If an alarm did not ring as expected, check its scheduled time, permissions, volume and Apple alarm controls. For issues, tell us whether the iPhone was locked, offline, in Silent Mode or in Focus mode.

For reliable operation, follow the in-app status and the alarm controls provided by iOS.

Subscriptions & Trials

When ROARISE Pro is available, trial eligibility, prices and renewal terms are shown in the app before purchase. Purchases are managed by Apple.

  • Cancel or manage subscriptions
  • Request a refund from Apple

Deleting ROARISE does not cancel an active subscription.

Restore Purchases

If you have purchased ROARISE Pro but it is not recognized, use the Restore Purchases option in the version of the app that offers subscriptions. Confirm you are using the correct Apple Account.

If that does not work, send a support request. Do not email full payment-card or Apple Account credentials.
